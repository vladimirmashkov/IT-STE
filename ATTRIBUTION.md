## Datasets and LLM systems

- Do not remove `rights`, `source_refs`, `translation_of`, or attribution metadata when transforming records.
- Retain project attribution in every exported item's metadata so that attribution survives dataset splitting.
- When displaying sourced material, show the project link, specific source, and applicable license in a manner appropriate to the interface.
- A link to IT-STE does not replace third-party attribution or terms. The license for our annotations does not become the license for a source standard.

These are practical instructions for retaining provenance and attribution. They do not determine the legal status of model training, model weights, or possible LLM output, and they do not expand the license terms.
