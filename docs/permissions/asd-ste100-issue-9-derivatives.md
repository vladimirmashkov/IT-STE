# ASD-STE100 Issue 9 derivative publication permission

[Русский](asd-ste100-issue-9-derivatives.ru.md)

The repository owner records that written authorization was received from the ASD-STE100 rights holder to use and publish derivative files made from ASD-STE100 Issue 9. The authorization was received as an offline written response; no public URL or digital copy is stored in this repository.

## Recorded scope

- Publication of machine-readable derivative files, including the structured SQLite retrieval database, is permitted.
- Publication of the source PDF is not permitted.
- The authorization does not make an IT-STE extraction, index, interpretation, translation, or adaptation official ASD guidance.
- No broader downstream license terms are recorded. Users must not infer permission to redistribute the source PDF or rights beyond the published derivative artifact.

## Repository policy

The source PDF remains under `.local/` and is excluded from Git. A published derivative must:

1. identify ASD as the owner and publisher of ASD-STE100;
2. identify the exact source issue and SHA-256;
3. include its own SHA-256 and generation metadata;
4. retain physical PDF page references and extraction warnings;
5. link to the IT-STE project at https://github.com/vladimirmashkov/IT-STE;
6. state that the derivative is mechanical, unreviewed, and not official ASD guidance.

This file records the repository owner's authorization claim and its operational limits. It is not a reproduction of the authorization letter and does not replace its terms. The original offline response must be retained by the repository owner.
