Alternatives and decision: Resolve both mechanical blockers and retain both as `restricted` draft senses. For `another`, include the verified source distinctions in the IT definition and use `one more`, `a different`, or `a further occurrence of` when the intended relation matters. Reject `allowed` because an unqualified use can mean addition, a different object, or a further occurrence; reject `prohibited` because the word is useful when the relation is explicit. For `anticlockwise`, retain a physical/graphical rotation sense, require the viewing direction or coordinate system, and offer `counterclockwise` as the contextual alternative. Reject a sequence-traversal sense; reject `allowed` because the reference frame can be omitted or differ between UI and physical contexts. These are draft editorial inferences: the source findings are facts, while the IT restrictions and examples are proposed applications of the evidence.
