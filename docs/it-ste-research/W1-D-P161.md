Scope is the stated adverb and adjective senses in software documentation, automation, networking, and procedures; this does not decide the related verb, noun, or `alternatively` entries.

Alternatives and decision: Resolve both mechanical blockers and retain both as `restricted` draft senses. For `D-P161-005`, use `alternately` only when two named operations or states occur in a stated order or cycle; reject the choice sense (use `alternatively` or name the choice). For `D-P161-006`, use `alternative` only for an explicitly identified route, method, or material offered in place of a stated option, with relevant conditions; reject use for alternating execution. `allowed` was rejected because both words can be ambiguous without the stated constraints; `prohibited` was rejected because the verified source senses are useful when made explicit; `needs-review` is no longer justified after the PDF boundary check. These are draft editorial inferences, not vocabulary approval.
